Setup — zero to paper-trading bot

01
Get free Alpaca keys

Sign up at alpaca.markets (paper account is free, no funding needed) and generate API keys.

02
Backtest first 
export ALPACA_KEY=... ALPACA_SECRET=...
python3 backtest.py

The pass bar: believable Sharpe, a drawdown you could sit through, enough trades to mean something. Fail here → it goes nowhere near money.

03
Paper trade for 2+ weeks 
python3 bot.py --loop

Fake money, real prices. Watch state/trades.csv — fills, stops, and behavior you didn't expect.

04
Daily briefings 
python3 briefing.py morning   # 7am cron
python3 briefing.py evening   # 9pm cron

Two messages a day — add TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN + TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID to get them on your phone.

05
Keep it running

Any $5/mo VPS or a spare machine: python3 bot.py --loop under systemd/tmux, or one python3 bot.py per 15-min cron tick.

Read this first. Educational project. Trading involves real financial risk and past performance doesn't guarantee anything. These are textbook strategies — assume the edge is modest at best and verify it yourself in the backtest and on paper. Going live requires deliberately editing config.py (LIVE_TRADING_ACKNOWLEDGED) and setting ALPACA_ENV=live — the bot ships locked to the paper account, and it should stay there until the data earns otherwise.
