Scoring rubric — 0 to 100

ComponentMaxWhat it measures
Business Quality25Moat, competitive position, revenue durability, unit economics trend
Financial Health25Cash vs. debt, FCF trajectory, burn rate, survivability without dilution
Valuation20Price vs. reasonable value range; what growth is already priced in
Hype vs. Reality15Fundamentals or narrative? Does the thesis survive a 40% drop?
Risk Profile15Severity and probability of the 3–6 biggest real risks
75–100 · BUY-CASEStrong business, reasonable price, clear 5-year thesis
50–74 · WATCH-CASEGood business, but too expensive or something needs proving
0–49 · AVOID-CASEBroken business, unreasonable valuation, or risk outweighs reward

Data rules — non-negotiable

  • All financial figures come from the company's own filings, earnings releases, and IR pages — never news articles.
  • Every number gets a source and period label (e.g. "Q1 FY26, 10-Q filed 2026-05-08").
  • Anything unverifiable is marked [UNVERIFIED] and excluded from scoring.
  • Every risk ends with a concrete, checkable sell trigger — never "if things deteriorate."
  • A falling price is not a sell signal; if the business isn't broken, it may be a reason to buy more.
